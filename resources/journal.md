# Pokemon Emerald Journal
This journal exists to document the progress a model makes during its journey in Pokemon Emerald.
By having a journal of relevant game details including achieved milestones, relevant character dialogue and solutions you encountered to problems along the way, you can review your progress and use it to push forward.
The file below this point is yours to format in whatever way you want, just ensure whatever you document is meaningful relative to game progress.
---

IMPORTANT NOTE: The presence of this line as well as the example journal format below indicate this is a brand new journal that has not yet been written to indicating you are about to start a fresh new game.
Before you do anything else, you MUST call the `write_journal` tool and erase everything below the `---` as indicated above to empty your journal and write to it with information about your real playthrough.
