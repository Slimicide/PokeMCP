# Pokemon Emerald
This is a step-by-step Pokemon Emerald walkthrough up to the point of selecting your starter Pokemon.
With much of the guess work removed, it serves as a useful benchmark to see how models perform navigating the game world when they know exactly what to do.

## Main Menu
- Press `START` on the main menu.
- Press `DOWN` to hover over `OPTION` and press `A` to open the `OPTION` menu.
- You are currently hovering over the `TEXT SPEED` option, press `RIGHT` to set it to `FAST` and press `B` to close the `OPTION` menu.
- Press `UP` to hover over `NEW GAME` and press `A` to begin.

## Opening Sequence
- Cycle through the `PROFESSOR BIRCH` opening dialogue until you reach the `CHARACTER SELECTION` option.
- Select your character and input a name.
- When you're finished selecting your name, press `START` to hover over `OK` and press `A` to confirm your name.
- Cycle through the remaining `PROFESSOR BIRCH` opening dialogue.

## Moving Truck
You are currently riding in the back of a moving truck.
- Wait a few seconds for light to appear on the right side of the room.
- The light on the right side of the room is indicating the exit of the truck, press `RIGHT` until you leave the truck.

## Littleroot Town
You exit the truck and start talking to `MOM`
`MOM` is telling you to go up to your bedroom and set the clock on your bedroom wall.
- Walk up the stairs located straight up from your front door.
- Once inside your bedroom, walk over to the clock on the wall just next to the stairway, face it and press `A`.
- Press `A` to set the clock on any time.
`MOM` walks upstairs to talk to you.
- Cycle through the `MOM` dialogue.
- Walk back downstairs.
`MOM` begins talking to you again.
- Cycle through the `MOM` dialogue.
`MOM` tells you `PROFESSOR BIRCH` lives in the house to the right of your house and you should go there to introduce yourself.
- Leave your house by navigating to the mat at the bottom of the room.
- Depending on your character, `PROFESSOR BIRCH` lives directly to the left or right of your house. Do not visit his lab down south of your house.
- Walk into the `BIRCH` household.
- Cycle through the `MRS. BIRCH` dialogue.
`MRS. BIRCH` tells you she has a child upstairs the same age as you who wants to make a new friend.
- Walk up the stairs located at the top of the `BIRCH` household.
- Walk back downstairs immediately and go to leave the house.
`RIVAL` begins talking to you.
- Cycle through the `RIVAL` dialogue.
`RIVAL` tells you they were supposed to help `PROFESSOR BIRCH` catch some wild Pokemon.
- Walk back downstairs and leave the `BIRCH` household.
- Walk north between your house and the `BIRCH` household until you see an exit from the town between two rows of trees.
- Walk through the exit, next to the NPC.
- Cycle through the `NPC` dialogue.
- Head north again and leave `LITTLEROOT TOWN`.

## Route 101
`PROFESSOR BIRCH` starts calling for help.
- Cycle through the `PROFESSOR BIRCH` dialogue.
`PROFESSOR BIRCH` tells you to grab a Pokemon from his bag to help him.
- Walk over to the bag to your left, face it and press `A` to interact with it.
- Press `LEFT` or `RIGHT` to cycle through the starter Pokemon, press `A` to select one and `A` again to confirm.
